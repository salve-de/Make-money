# Research and Competitors

## 1. Direct and adjacent competitors

| Category | Examples | Strength | Gap to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business ideas | IdeaBrowser, Trends.vc | Ideas and market reports | Weak connection to verified money and live services |
| Founder stories | Starter Story, Indie Hackers, Failory | Narrative and self-identification | Historical, fragmented evidence, weak market graph |
| Trend tools | Exploding Topics | Momentum discovery | Attention is not payment; weak addressability |
| Company data | Crunchbase, Dealroom, STARTUP DB | Funding and company research | Funding is not customer demand; enterprise-centric |
| Product discovery | Product Hunt | Strong launch participation | Launch attention, not durable opportunity intelligence |
| Review/comparison | G2, ITreview, AlternativeTo | Buyer use case and vendor pages | Focus on selecting known categories, not creating businesses |
| Deals | AppSumo | Buyer demand and vendor acquisition | Deal-driven, weak market intelligence |
| M&A | Acquire, ラッコM&A | Actual listings and transactions | Focus on buying/selling existing assets |
| Government spend | USAspending, TED, Gビズインフォ | Authoritative spending data | Hard to translate into small-business entry paths |

## 3. Lessons

### Product Hunt

投稿者と閲覧者の両方が必要。自由投稿をHomeと同一にすると品質が崩れるため、submission poolとcurated feedを分離する。

### G2 / ITreview

BuyerとVendorを接続し、Vendorへanalyticsを売れる。ただしレビュー・関係者・スポンサーの透明性が必要。

### Indie Hackers

Productに収益、創業者、進捗を接続すると、単なるリンク集より自己接続が強くなる。

### M&A marketplaces

金額・利益・運営負荷・所有確認は強い関心を生む。一方、取引まで自社で担うと本人確認、契約、エスクロー、紛争対応が急増する。
