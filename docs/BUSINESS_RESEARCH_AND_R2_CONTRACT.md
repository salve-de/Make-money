- the `research-bundle.v1` -> Journal -> typed/derived/view path;
- existing R2 roles, create-only/readback rules, and the legacy-data boundary;
- the flexible product-view boundary and scale/serving constraints;
- completion, publication, and reporting gates.

This file is only a local discovery pointer. It does not define a new schema, dataset, R2 bucket, prefix, or write method. The Make-Money execution entrypoint remains [`COLLECT_AND_STORE.md`](COLLECT_AND_STORE.md).
